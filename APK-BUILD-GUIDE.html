📱 FullTime Android APK Guide

Follow these steps to convert your FullTime site into a real Android APK using PWABuilder (free).

PART 1 — Upload Files to Your Hosting First

Make sure these files are uploaded to instantlivefootball.com.ng:

  • ✅ index.html
  • ✅ sw.js (service worker)
  • ✅ manifest.json
  • ✅ icons/ folder with all icon PNG files
⚠️ The icons folder is important. First open generate-icons.html in your browser, tap "Generate & Download All Icons", then upload them all into a folder called icons on your hosting.

PART 2 — Build the APK with PWABuilder

1Go to PWABuilder
https://www.pwabuilder.com
2Enter your site URL

Type your URL and click Start:

https://instantlivefootball.com.ng
3Wait for the scan

PWABuilder will scan your site. You should see a high score (green). Click Next.

4Choose Android package

On the "Publish" page, find the Android card and click Generate.

5Configure your app

Fill in these details:

  • App name: FullTime
  • Package ID: com.instantlivefootball.app
  • App version: 1.0
  • Version code: 1
  • Display mode: standalone
  • Status bar color: #07090a
  • Nav bar color: #07090a
  • Splash screen color: #07090a
6Download the APK

Click Download. You'll get a ZIP file. Inside it is your FullTime.apk file.

📦 The ZIP also contains signing keys and a bundle. Use the .apk file for direct installation. Use the .aab bundle if you want to upload to Google Play Store.

PART 3 — Share Your APK

7Host the APK for download

Upload the APK to your hosting at:

instantlivefootball.com.ng/FullTime.apk

Then users can download and install it directly. Add this button to your site:

<a href="/FullTime.apk" download>Download Android App</a>
8Or share via WhatsApp / Telegram

You can also send the APK file directly via WhatsApp or Telegram to your users. They tap it to install.

⚠️ Users need to enable "Install from unknown sources" in their Android settings. Tell them: Settings → Security → Install unknown apps → Allow

PART 4 — iOS (iPhone)

iPhones can install your app as a PWA without an APK:

  1. Open instantlivefootball.com.ng in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (box with arrow)
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add — app icon appears on home screen!
